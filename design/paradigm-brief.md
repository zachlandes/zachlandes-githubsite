# zachlandes.com: paradigm brief

Settled briefly rather than through a full paradigm-grill, as firstmate's brief for this task directed.
Sources: the captain's ask and the trolley sharing plan, both relayed by firstmate on 2026-10-02.
Any group marked `looked up` names where the fact came from; nothing here is invented biography.

## 1. The job

Status: answered (captain's ask).
Zachary wants a home for his writing about AI on his own name, for personal reputation and for people in AI circles to engage with.
It is not a consulting funnel.
Today the domain shows a 2024 portfolio template with leftover placeholder services, testimonials and portfolio items, which undercuts any article linked from it.

## 2. The moment of use

Status: answered (sharing plan).
A reader arrives from a Hacker News, Reddit, LinkedIn or Bluesky link, most first clicks on a phone.
They are curious and skeptical, with a few minutes, and will leave the moment the page is slow, noisy or asks for anything.

## 3. The 90 seconds before

Status: answered (sharing plan).
They saw a link card (title, description, preview image) or an HN title, and tapped it.
They arrive holding that title and a question about whether the claim holds up.

## 4. The 90 seconds after

Status: answered.
Read the article, maybe open the about section to see who wrote it, maybe scan the post list, maybe subscribe by RSS.
Comments happen elsewhere (HN, Reddit, LinkedIn); the site has none.

## 5. Frequency and lifespan

Status: answered.
Most readers visit once, for one article.
A few return through RSS when the next essay appears (the next planned one is about software engineering with AI agents, from an attention-and-focus angle after Oliver Burkeman's Four Thousand Weeks).
Posts accumulate slowly: a handful a year.

## 7. What is genuinely locked

Status: answered.

Locked:
- Served by GitHub Pages from the `master` branch root, custom domain zachlandes.com via `CNAME`; no DNS, Cloudflare or Pages settings change.
- Static output only.
- An RSS feed from day one.
- Title, description and preview image metadata on every page.
- Readable on a phone.
- No pop-ups, tracking banners, analytics, newsletter forms or comments.
- A post can be long-form interactive custom HTML (inline SVG, scripts, wide figures, tables, collapsible sections) as well as plain prose.
- The existing resume PDF stays, linked from the about section.
- Biographical copy comes only from the existing site and resume, and Zachary rewrites it.

Not locked:
- The current template, its jQuery, fonts, imagery and colours.
- Margin side-comments in posts.
- Which static generator, as long as Pages can serve it without a settings change.

## 8. The information architecture

Status: answered.
Nouns: one person (name, short about, links: email, LinkedIn, resume), a handful of posts (title, date, description, preview image, body), one feed.
At rest on the home page: the name, a sentence or two of about, the post list.
At rest on a post: the title, the date, and the opening of the prose with no chrome above it.

## 9. Success, failure, and the cost of error

Status: answered.
Success: an HN reader on a phone reads the article start to finish, the link card looks right everywhere it is pasted, and someone subscribes or follows up.
Failure: a slow or broken page on launch day, a mangled figure on a phone, or a page that looks like a template; HN does not give a second chance.
